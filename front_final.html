About Us

Our Mission

This web application recognizes Indian Sign Language in real-time using cutting-edge technologies like Mediapipe and TensorFlow. Our goal is to bridge the communication gap between speech and sign language, making interactions more inclusive and accessible for everyone.

Meet Our Team Rule Breakers

We are Team Rule Breakers — a passionate group of innovators dedicated to breaking barriers in communication technology. Our team believes in challenging conventions and creating solutions that make a real difference in people's lives.

With diverse expertise in AI, machine learning, web development, and user experience design, we've come together to revolutionize how sign language is recognized and learned. We're not just building technology; we're building bridges between communities.

Future Scope

Our vision extends far beyond the current implementation. We're committed to continuous innovation and expansion:

Multi-Language Support: Expanding beyond Indian Sign Language to include ASL, BSL, and other regional sign languages, creating a truly global platform.

Mobile Applications: Developing native iOS and Android apps for on-the-go sign language recognition and learning.

AI-Powered Learning: Implementing adaptive learning algorithms that personalize the learning experience based on individual progress and learning styles.

Gamification: Creating engaging games and challenges to make learning sign language fun and interactive for all age groups.

Community Features: Building a social platform where learners can connect, practice together, and share their sign language journey.

Healthcare Integration: Partnering with healthcare providers to facilitate better communication between medical professionals and hearing-impaired patients.
